About Baked By Sihle

Family-owned, community-loved - where tradition meets taste.

Established in 2023, Baked By Sihle began as a home-based micro-bakery with a single sourdough starter. Today, we're a beloved local bakery serving fresh, handcrafted goods daily.

Our mission is to delight our community with exceptional, freshly baked products made from quality ingredients. We believe in slow fermentation, local grains, and the joy of sharing bread.

Vision: To be the heart of our neighbourhood, where tradition meets taste.

Our values

  • 100% natural sourdough
  • Family recipes, modern twist
  • Sustainable packaging
